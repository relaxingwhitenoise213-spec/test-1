## 7. Creative (this decides 80% of performance)

Formats: 4:5 for feeds, 9:16 for Stories/Reels. Test 2–3 videos + 1–2 statics per ad set. UGC-style phone footage beats polished studio video in this niche.

**Video hooks (first 3 seconds):**
1. Dog furiously shaking head in slow-mo → text overlay: "If your dog does THIS… watch this." → demo → happy calm dog.
2. "STOP cleaning your pet's ears with wipes/Q-tips" → why → product demo.
3. POV: satisfying cleaning demo → cotton pad reveal → relieved pup. Keep the "dirty" shot mild — extreme gross-out closeups get flagged as shocking content.
4. Talking-head pet-parent testimonial: problem → tried everything → this worked.

**Statics:** clean product shot + benefit checklist; "before: scratching / after: sleeping" split (imply comfort, not medical cure).

## 8. Budget, KPIs, kill/scale rules

**Unit economics (at $24.95 retail):** landed cost ≈ $8–10 + fees ≈ $1.50 → ~$14 margin → break-even CPA ≈ $14, break-even ROAS ≈ 1.8. Add a bundle ("Buy 2, Get 1 Free" — multi-pet households are common) to push AOV and target blended ROAS 2.5+.

**USA pet-niche benchmarks:** CPM $15–35 · CTR ≥ 1.5% (good ≥ 2%) · CPC ≤ $1.20 · Cost per ATC ≤ $5.

**Rules:**
- Kill an ad at ~$15 spend with 0 add-to-carts, or CTR < 1% with CPC > $1.50.
- Kill an ad set at ~1.5× break-even CPA spent with no purchase.
- Scale winners +20% budget every 48h (don't double overnight — it resets learning).
- At ~50 total purchases: duplicate winning creatives into one Advantage+ Shopping Campaign at $100+/day and let ABO ad sets wind down.
- Refresh creative every 2–3 weeks; frequency > 3 on retargeting = rotate.

**Landing page checklist:** own store (not AliExpress), 4–6 reviews with pet photos, GIF/video of application above the fold, bundle offer, guarantee badge, realistic shipping times stated.
