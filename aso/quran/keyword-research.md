# Keyword Research & Competitor Analysis — Quran & Athan App

Research date: July 2026. Method: App Store search-intelligence sweep of the US, UK, Arabic (SA/AE/EG)
and Indonesian storefronts, plus category roundups. (Apple blocks automated scraping, so competitor
keyword *fields* are inferred from live Titles/Subtitles and category behaviour.)

---

## The market in one paragraph

This is a **large, loyal, and highly seasonal** category dominated by a few giants — **Muslim Pro**
(190M+ downloads, Bitsmedia), **Athan Pro** (Quanticapps), **IslamicFinder Athan**, **Muslim** (Muslim
Inc., "60M+"), and specialist **Quran** apps (Quran Majeed, Al Quran GTAF, Quran.com). They own the
English head terms. Your openings: (1) **under-served languages** — Indonesian (biggest Muslim market)
and Arabic (one localization → ~20 storefronts) where the Western brands localize less thoroughly;
(2) **long-tail + transliteration variants** the giants can't all fit in 100 characters; and
(3) the **Ramadan wave**, where fresh, well-optimized listings ride a 5–10× traffic spike.

## Competitor title patterns (what wins)

| App | Title (observed) | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **Muslim Pro** | `Muslim Pro: Quran & Athan` | Brand + the two biggest nouns. In Indonesia it localizes to `Muslim Pro: Jadwal Sholat Azan`. |
| **Athan Pro** | `Athan Pro: Muslim Prayer Times` | Leads brand with "Athan", pairs "Muslim Prayer Times". ID: `Athan Pro: Quran, Azan, Kiblat`. |
| **Athan (IslamicFinder)** | `Athan: Prayer Times & Al Quran` | ID: `Athan: Jadwal Sholat, Al Quran`; AR: `اذان: اوقات الصلاة، قرآن، قبلة`. |
| **Muslim (Muslim Inc.)** | `Muslim: Ramadan 2026, Azan` | Rotates **Ramadan + year** into the title seasonally — copy this tactic. |
| **Sajda** | `Sajda: Prayer times, Quran` | Clean brand-first pattern. |

**Takeaway:** `Brand: [Quran] & [Athan/Prayer Times]` in the title, then spread Qibla / Salah / Namaz /
Dua / Tasbeeh / Hijri and the **spelling variants** into subtitle + keyword field. Localize the *nouns*
per market (Athan→Adzan, Salah→Sholat, Qibla→Kiblat).

## Keyword universe per locale

Legend: **[T]** Title · **[S]** Subtitle · **[K]** Keyword field. Everything else = rotate-in candidate.
Apple combines single words across fields, so value is in the combinations (right column).

### 🇺🇸 English (U.S.)
Placed: **quran[T] athan[T]** · **prayer[S] times[S] qibla[S] salah[S]** · **azan[K] adhan[K] namaz[K]
muslim[K] islam[K] dua[K] ramadan[K] hijri[K] calendar[K] tasbeeh[K] dhikr[K] surah[K] tajweed[K]
mosque[K] compass[K]**
Combinations: *quran & athan, prayer times, salah/namaz times, azan/adhan alarm, qibla compass,
muslim/islamic app, hijri calendar, islamic calendar, quran surah, quran tajweed, ramadan, dua/dhikr,
tasbeeh counter.*
Rotate-in: `koran, masjid, mecca, medina, fajr, reciter, tafsir, murattal, eid, zakat, iftar, suhoor,
sunnah, hadith, kaaba, halal, salat, solat, prayer alarm`.

### 🇬🇧 English (U.K.)  — lean into "namaz" (large South-Asian Muslim population)
Placed: **quran[T] athan[T]** · **prayer[S] times[S] namaz[S] qibla[S]** · **azan[K] adhan[K] salah[K]
salat[K] muslim[K] islam[K] dua[K] ramadan[K] hijri[K] calendar[K] tasbeeh[K] dhikr[K] surah[K]
tajweed[K] masjid[K]**
Why different from US: UK deliberately puts **namaz** in the subtitle and adds **salah, salat, masjid**;
US carries **namaz, mosque, compass** in its field. Across both English storefronts you cover every variant.
Rotate-in: `koran, mecca, fajr, reciter, tafsir, eid, zakat, solat, prayer alarm, qibla compass`.

### 🇸🇦 Arabic — one localization serves ALL Arabic storefronts (SA, AE, EG, QA, KW, …)
Placed: **القرآن[T] الأذان[T]** · **مواقيت[S] الصلاة[S] القبلة[S] الأذكار[S]** · **قران[K] اذان[K] مسلم[K]
اسلام[K] دعاء[K] رمضان[K] تسبيح[K] مصحف[K] تفسير[K] تلاوة[K] هجري[K] تقويم[K] بوصلة[K] مسجد[K] ختمة[K]
زكاة[K] سبحة[K] سورة[K]**
Note the **spelling-variant play**: the keyword field includes the *no-hamza* forms **قران** and **اذان**
(how millions actually type on phones) even though the title uses the formal **القرآن / الأذان**.
Combinations: *مواقيت الصلاة, وقت الأذان, اتجاه القبلة, القرآن الكريم, تلاوة القرآن, التقويم الهجري, أذكار
المسلم, دعاء, تسبيح, ختمة القرآن.*
Rotate-in (esp. Ramadan): `صيام, سحور, افطار, تراويح, ليلة القدر, يس, الكهف, مكة, المدينة, حصن, قبلة`.

### 🇮🇩 Indonesian — biggest Muslim market; use LOCAL spellings (adzan, sholat, kiblat)
Placed: **quran[T] adzan[T]** · **jadwal[S] sholat[S] kiblat[S] doa[S]** · **azan[K] waktu[K] shalat[K]
muslim[K] islami[K] dzikir[K] ramadhan[K] puasa[K] imsak[K] murottal[K] tajwid[K] kompas[K] alquran[K]
tasbih[K]**
Combinations: *jadwal sholat, waktu sholat, adzan/azan, arah kiblat, al quran / alquran, murottal, tajwid,
kompas kiblat, dzikir & doa, ramadhan puasa imsak.*
Rotate-in: `sahur, yasin, asmaul, husna, terjemahan, ngaji, hijriah, masjid, tahajud, wirid, sholawat,
tarawih, iftar, buka puasa`.

## Trademark / rejection guardrails (applied)
- ❌ No competitor brand names in metadata ("Muslim Pro", "Athan Pro", "IslamicFinder", "Quran Majeed").
- ❌ No duplicate tokens within a locale; keyword field has **no spaces**, single words only.
- ✅ Respectful, accurate religious terminology; no unverifiable claims.
- ✅ Localized *nouns* per market — the whole point of this niche.
