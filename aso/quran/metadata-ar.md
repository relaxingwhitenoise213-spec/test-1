## Notes (for the non-Arabic reader)

  (Athan) — how most people actually type on a phone keyboard — while the title uses the formal
  **القرآن / الأذان**. That captures both spellings.
- Keyword glosses: مسلم=Muslim, اسلام=Islam, دعاء=dua, رمضان=Ramadan, تسبيح=tasbih, مصحف=Mushaf,
  تفسير=tafsir, تلاوة=recitation, هجري=Hijri, تقويم=calendar, بوصلة=compass, مسجد=mosque, ختمة=Quran
  completion, زكاة=zakat, سبحة=prayer beads, سورة=surah.
