# Keyword Research & Competitor Analysis — Oran

Research date: July 2026. Method: App Store search-intelligence sweep of the US, UK, FR and BR
storefronts + top-charts/editorial roundups. (Apple blocks automated scraping of the store
pages, so exact competitor keyword *fields* are inferred from their live Titles/Subtitles and
category behaviour, not read directly.)

---

## The market in one paragraph

The AI-assistant category is huge and top-heavy. The **head terms** ("ai", "chatbot", "ai chat",
"assistant") are dominated by billion-download brands — **ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity,
Grok, Claude** — plus a tier of aggressive indie "wrapper" apps (**ChatOn, Nova, Genie, Chatbot
App, Chat Smith**) that spend heavily and iterate ASO constantly. A brand-new app like Oran will
**not** rank on page 1 for "ai chatbot" in week one. The winnable game is: (1) **local-language
storefronts** where the big US brands localise less aggressively, and (2) **long-tail
combinations** ("essay writer", "image generator", "homework helper", "pdf chat", "assistant IA",
"gerador de imagem"). That's exactly how this kit is built.

## Direct competitors (indie "all-in-one AI" tier — your real comp set)

| App | Title pattern observed | Takeaway for Oran |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatOn** | `ChatOn – AI Chat Bot Assistant` | Front-loads *AI + Chat + Bot + Assistant*; brand first. ~100M users. |
| **Nova** | `AI Chatbot – Nova` / `IA Chatbot – Nova` (BR) | Same skeleton you already use; localises the *keyword* half per market. |
| **Genie** | `Chatbot AI Assistant – Genie` / `IA Chatbot Français – Genie AI` (FR) | Puts the language word ("Français") right in the title abroad. |
| **Chatbot App** | `Chatbot App: AI Assistant` / `Assistente de IA` (BR) | Pure-keyword title, brand *is* the keyword. |
| **Chat Smith** | `Chat Smith: IA Chat Português` (BR) | Uses "Português" as a title keyword. |

**Pattern that wins:** `[Brand] – [AI] [Chatbot/Chat] [Assistant/Bot] [+ language word abroad]`.
Oran already fits this; we're tightening the keyword tokens and, crucially, **localising** it.

## Head brands (aspirational, not week-1 targets)

ChatGPT · Google Gemini · Microsoft Copilot · Perplexity · Grok · Claude. You compete with these
on **workflow/price/simplicity**, not on head-term ranking. Do **not** put their names in your
metadata (trademark rejection risk).

## Keyword universe per locale

Legend: **[T]** used in Title, **[S]** in Subtitle, **[K]** in Keyword field. Everything else is
a candidate you can rotate in later. Remember Apple auto-combines across fields, so the value is
in the *combinations* (right-hand column).

### 🇺🇸 English (U.S.) — storefront: United States
Placed: **oran[T] ai[T] chatbot[T] assistant[T]** · **ask[S] bot[S] chat[S] write[S] art[S]** ·
**writer[K] essay[K] homework[K] study[K] grammar[K] translate[K] image[K] generator[K] voice[K]
pdf[K] answer[K] genius[K] personal[K] photo[K]**

High-value combinations this unlocks: *ai chatbot · ai assistant · chat bot · ai art · ai image ·
image generator · photo generator · essay writer · ai writer · homework helper(→ helper in UK) ·
grammar assistant · voice assistant · personal assistant · chat pdf · ask ai · translate*

Strong candidates to rotate in later (via Search-terms data): `keyboard, email, resume, story,
poem, code, summarize, girlfriend(no), friend, brain, robot, math, solver`.

### 🇬🇧 English (U.K.) — storefront: United Kingdom
Placed: **oran[T] ai[T] chatbot[T] assistant[T]** · **essay[S] writer[S] art[S] images[S]** ·
**chat[K] bot[K] ask[K] homework[K] study[K] grammar[K] translate[K] voice[K] generator[K] pdf[K]
answer[K] helper[K] personal[K] smart[K] code[K]**

Why a *separate* UK listing: it's an independent keyword field, so we deliberately **diversify** —
UK carries `helper, smart, code` which the US field couldn't fit. Across both English storefronts
you now cover more ground. (British spelling differences are minor for these tech terms:
"personalise/optimise" etc. don't appear in our keywords, so no spelling split needed.)

### 🇫🇷 French (France) — storefront: France
Placed: **oran[T] chatbot[T] ia[T] français[T]** · **assistant[S] chat[S] art[S] image[S]** ·
**écrire[K] traduction[K] générateur[K] devoirs[K] résumé[K] voix[K] grammaire[K] dessin[K]
intelligence[K] artificielle[K] réponse[K]**

Combinations: *chatbot IA · assistant IA · IA français · intelligence artificielle · générateur
image (→ générateur d'image) · générateur art · traduction · écrire · devoirs (homework) · résumé
· grammaire · dessin (drawing/art) · réponse*. Note: **"IA" is the French word for AI** and
**"Français" is itself a high-volume keyword** ("chatbot français") — both belong in the title,
exactly as top FR competitors do. Rotate-in candidates: `rédaction, traducteur, dissertation,
robot, texte, correcteur, orthographe, mail, CV`.

### 🇧🇷 Portuguese (Brazil) — storefront: Brazil  ⟵ biggest volume opportunity

Combinations: *chatbot IA · assistente de IA · inteligência artificial · IA português · gerador

### 🇵🇹 Portuguese (Portugal) — storefront: Portugal
Placed: **oran[T] chatbot[T] ia[T] português[T]** · **assistente[S] chat[S] arte[S] imagem[S]** ·
**portugal[K] escrever[K] redação[K] tradutor[K] gerador[K] estudo[K] resumo[K] voz[K] gramática[K]
inteligência[K] artificial[K] foto[K]**

Same language as Brazil but a **separate storefront**, so it gets its own field. Swapped `brasil→
portugal`, and `tarefa/desenho→estudo/foto` to widen coverage. PT-PT vocabulary note: Portugal
tends to say *"trabalhos de casa"* for homework and *"aplicação"* for app (Brazil: *"tarefa"* /
*"aplicativo"*) — reflected in the description, not needed in the single-word keyword field.

## Trademark / rejection guardrails (applied throughout)

- ❌ Not used anywhere in Title/Subtitle/Keywords: **chatgpt, gpt, openai, gemini, bard, claude,
  grok, deepseek, copilot, llama**. Using another company's trademark in metadata is an App
  Review rejection (4.1 Copycats / 5.2 Intellectual Property) and an easy takedown target.
- ❌ No plurals-of-a-word-you-already-have (Apple stems most plurals; e.g. don't buy both
  `image` and `images` in the *same* field).
- ❌ No spaces in the keyword field, no repeated tokens (each wastes characters for zero gain).
- ✅ Generic descriptors of capability ("latest AI models", "advanced AI") are fine and used in
  the descriptions.
