## Where else to put the Terms & Conditions URL

| Location | Field |
|---|---|
| App Store Connect | App Information → License Agreement (or leave Apple's standard EULA) and the **Privacy Policy URL** field (a privacy policy URL is mandatory — host one at the same site, e.g. `/privacy-policy.html`) |
| Google Play Console | Store presence → App content → **Privacy policy** (mandatory); T&C link belongs in the description as above |
| In-app | Settings screen → "Terms & Conditions" link (both stores expect legal links to be reachable inside the app too) |

> Note: both stores **require a privacy policy URL** as a separate field — a terms-and-conditions page alone will not pass review. If you don't have one yet, publish a privacy policy page alongside the T&C page and use that URL in the console fields.
