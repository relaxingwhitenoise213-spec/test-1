# TikTok Ads Pack — Panoramic Bulb Security Camera (US, $30/day)

TikTok ≠ Meta. Key differences that change your playbook:
- Ad text is **max 100 characters**, no headline/description fields → the video carries everything.
- CPMs are cheaper (~$8–15 US ecom vs $25–50 on Meta) but clicks are more impulsive and convert worse on first touch.
- Creative fatigues faster — plan a fresh video batch weekly.
- Polished "ads" flop. Native, raw, phone-shot content wins. **Don't make ads, make TikToks.**

## Campaign setup ($30/day)

1. Install the **TikTok app in Shopify** → Pixel + Events API, Advanced Matching ON. Test that Complete Payment fires with value.
2. Structure:
```
Campaign: Website Conversions (leave campaign budget OPEN — campaign-level budget has a $50/day minimum)
└── 1 Ad group — $30/day (ad group minimum is $20/day)
    ├── Optimization: Complete Payment
    ├── Placement: TikTok ONLY — turn OFF Pangle + Global App Bundle
    ├── Targeting: USA, age 25–55+, all genders, NO interest targeting (broad + Smart Audience on)
    └── 3–5 videos, each with 1 ad text
```
3. **Comments:** dropshipping reality — commenters will post "this is $20 on AliExpress." Either disable comments at the ad level for launch, or keep them on (better engagement signal) and hide/filter hostile ones daily in the moderation tools. Pick one and actually do it.
4. **Spark Ads (do this):** make a TikTok account for the brand, post your 3 videos organically, then run them as Spark Ads (authorize via video code). Spark ads show your handle, collect likes/comments that persist, and consistently outperform dark posts for dropshipping.
5. Same kill rules as the Meta guide (`meta-ads-guide.md` §6), with one change: judge CTR against ~1.5%+ (TikTok CTRs run higher than Meta; under 1% on TikTok = dead creative).

## The 3 video scripts (15–30s, 9:16, phone-shot, captions on everything)

Hook rule on TikTok: you have **1.5 seconds**, not 3. Open mid-action, never with a logo or product box.

### Script 1 — "Install wow" (angle: 30-second setup / no fees)
| Time | Shot | On-screen text / VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Hand screwing a "light bulb" into porch socket, already mid-motion | "This is NOT a light bulb…" |
| 2–5s | Phone opens app → live 360° pan of the yard | "…it's a 360° security camera 🤯" |
| 5–10s | Quick cuts: motion alert pops on phone; night vision clip; zoom | "Motion alerts • Night vision • Full panoramic view" |
| 10–15s | Talking to a delivery driver through it, driver waves | "You can even talk through it" |
| 15–22s | Side-by-side: "$200+ system + monthly fees" vs "this, one time" | "No wiring. No electrician. No subscription." |
| 22–28s | Screw-in shot again, app open, thumbs up | "30-second install. 50% off today — link in bio button 👇" |

## Godmode rules for TikTok

1. **First frame = mid-action.** Screwing in the bulb, alert buzzing, figure on the porch. Never a logo, never a product on a table.
2. Captions on every spoken word (most watch muted-ish); use TikTok's native-style bold captions.
3. Voiceover: real human voice or TikTok's TTS voice — the TTS voice weirdly boosts native feel; test both.
4. Cut every 1–2 seconds. A static 5-second shot is death on TikTok.
5. Refresh weekly: same 3 angles, new hooks/first-2-seconds. TikTok fatigues in days, not weeks.
6. TikTok buyers are impulse buyers — the "no monthly fees vs $200 systems" value frame does the price justification your $74.95 needs. Lead with it more than on Meta.
7. Expect a worse on-platform ROAS than Meta at first + more "view-through" style delayed purchases: check Shopify orders against TikTok's numbers before killing anything.
8. Same compliance as Meta, one addition: no real crime/doorbell footage with identifiable faces, no "spy/hidden camera" framing — TikTok bans surveillance-of-others ads too. Keep every "porch pirate" staged.
