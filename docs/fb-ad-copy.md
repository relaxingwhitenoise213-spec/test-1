## Product-specific compliance (this niche gets accounts banned)

1. **Never use "spy cam", "hidden camera", or "watch them without them knowing"** — Meta bans promoting covert surveillance of people without consent. Frame everything as *home security* and *checking on your own home/pets/kids*.
2. Don't use real crime footage with identifiable faces — staged porch-pirate demos are safe, real doorbell-cam theft clips are a flag risk.
3. Only claim what your exact model does: if it's 2K, don't say 4K; only say "no monthly fees" if it genuinely records to SD/app without a subscription.
4. Review counts, star ratings, and sale deadlines must be real (Meta policy + FTC).
5. Keep the "protection" tone confident, not terrifying — graphic fear-based ads get rejected.
